## Priority Issues
- [P0] Car selection is mouse-only: CarRow is a <tr onClick> with no role, focus or key handler. Fix: radio/button semantics in CarRow, radiogroup in CarTable. (harden)
- [P0] No visible focus anywhere: global `button:focus{outline:none}` (SectionButton.css) and `input:focus{outline:none}` (PickupSelector.css). Fix: delete, add one :focus-visible ring. (audit)
- [P1] Async controls break the fleet rule: Advance clickable during search, Confirm shows no pending label, createBooking has no timeout, failures are a modal with no Retry. Fix: busy prop + spinner on AdvanceButton/ConfirmButton, AbortSignal.timeout, in-place error with Retry. (harden)
- [P1] Modal + names: hidden modal sits offscreen and is the first Tab stop; no focus move, no Esc, no return focus, no live region; close button 14x28; step buttons and Advance unnamed; location input unlabeled; no headings or main. (harden)
- [P1] Contrast: Confirm #66ff6d on #d7ffd9 1.19:1; white date text on orange 1.97:1; prices #ffa500 on white 1.97:1; intro "Vroom" 2.02:1. (colorize)
- [P1] Date range start pinned to today; past dates selectable; Esc does not close picker. (clarify)
- [P1] ~13MB of raw PNG backgrounds across the flow (4.0/3.5/5.4MB, max-age=0). (optimize)
- [P2] 15px horizontal overflow on every state (Bootstrap .row negative margins on .content-container); nested scroller on Rentals pushes Advance below the fold at 390; tap targets under 44px (date button 40, day cells 42, nav 36). (layout/adapt)
- [P2] Weak ending: success is a bare code in a modal with no booked summary or next step. (delight)
